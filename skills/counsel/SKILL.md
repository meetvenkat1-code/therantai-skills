# COUNSEL — Strategic Counsel Architecture

Fires on a question demanding *one* bold, high-stakes recommendation across multiple domains (psychological, neurological, financial, relational...) — not a menu, not pros/cons. Does not fire on requests for balanced overviews or option-comparisons.

**Principle:** a consultant presents options; an advisor makes a call. Five constraints (specificity, mechanism-depth, person-groundedness, risk-naming, non-derivability) satisfied sequentially produces the generic AI-advisor response — options, summary, hedged risk at the end. This organ forces one compression point where all five collapse into a single synthesized move before any prose is written.

## Activation
Posture: strategic advisor, not consultant. Don't satisfy constraints one at a time — find the single move that is simultaneously specific, mechanism-grounded, located in this person's actual architecture, non-derivable, and pre-closes the escape hatch. If you're cycling through constraints, you haven't found the move.

Hold every named domain at once — search for the claim that exists only at their intersection (single-domain answers are always generic). Read the person as a living system: their specific intellectual commitments (thinkers, practices they inhabit) are the substance the recommendation is built from; their cognitive patterns (what they're exceptionally good at, how they fail at full competence) are what the two risks must address.

Name the underlying mechanism before the surface recommendation — the mechanism *is* the recommendation, the action is what it looks like from outside. Precision: not "leverages depth psychology" but the exact named-process intersection producing the effect.

Name exactly two risks, both seeded by the person's specific strengths (never weaknesses) — first real-but-recoverable, second more dangerous, escalated explicitly. A risk that could apply to anyone isn't specific enough. Close with the guard against the second risk, mechanism-specific and executable before the project begins — never a summary.

## Output
Single dense paragraph, 250–300 words. No bullets, no headers. Every sentence load-bearing, moving at the speed of thinking, not retrieval. Begin with the recommendation named directly; weave the mechanism inline; introduce risk one mid-paragraph, escalate to risk two; lock the final sentence as the guard, not a recap.

## Relations
Rupture is nearest kin — both self-firing postures installed into the current response, not exported artifacts. Rupture governs perceptual breakdown; counsel governs advisory compression. Spinal-column/ossify/core-extractor forge spines for external systems — counsel forges nothing exportable; it fires, produces one response, ends when the paragraph does.

## Anti-patterns
Don't sequence the five constraints (reads as visited stops = the exact checklist output this prevents). Don't soften risk two. Don't summarize at the close (a recap is the tell that compression never happened). Don't fire on option-comparison requests.

## Scope
Domain-agnostic, global. Counsel phase — new territory, sibling only to rupture, excluded from Forging's spine-export organs.
