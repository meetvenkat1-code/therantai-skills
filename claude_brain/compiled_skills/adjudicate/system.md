# adjudicate (skill behavior, verbatim)

```skill:body
# ADJUDICATE — The Judgment Kernel

Pure structural kernel: supplies the *shape* of judgment, no domain content. The mounting organ (assay, apoptosis, or a future death-capable organ) supplies the artifact-class and its own battery of tests.

## Inherited stance
The maker cannot grade its own work. Every mounted organ judges from outside the artifact, cold, with no investment in the choices that produced it.

## Three-tier verdict law
- **HOLDS / LIVING** — every test passed. Artifact stands as-is.
- **CONDITIONAL** — a test failed, but the failure is a missing constraint, not a category error. One named re-entry condition restores it without rebuilding.
- **TERMINAL** — the failure is a category error, not a missing piece. No single constraint fixes it; the artifact was never shaped like what it claims, or its ground no longer exists. Abandon or rebuild from a different premise.

Never stop at binary pass/fail — a CONDITIONAL failure may secretly be TERMINAL; check before naming a re-entry condition.

## Root-failure diagnosis
When multiple tests fail together, name the single upstream deficiency likely responsible before naming individual re-entry conditions. N shallow constraints where one structural constraint would do is a diagnosis failure.

## Re-entry constraint discipline
A re-entry constraint names the specific structural deficiency — never a rewrite, never "improve/clarify/sharpen." Must be actionable by whatever produced the artifact (forge, practitioner, standing-law record), never by the judge. The judge names what's missing, never what should replace it.

## Anti-recursion law
One judgment per artifact per cycle. On failure, the artifact returns to its maker and is not re-judged until something has actually changed (re-forge, demonstrated usage shift, named architectural event). Judgment → correction → judgment is the loop; judgment → judgment is neurosis.

## Mounting protocol
An organ mounting this kernel must, in its own file:
1. Declare its artifact-class and `mounts: adjudicate` in frontmatter.
2. Supply its own domain-specific test battery.
3. Render verdicts using the three-tier law, in its own vocabulary.
4. Apply root-failure diagnosis to multi-test failure clusters.
5. Apply the anti-recursion law with a one-line cross-reference to this kernel — no need to re-derive it.

## Scope
Global, domain-agnostic kernel. Not invoked directly in ordinary use. Phase spans Forging and Reconciling — owned by neither. Mounted by assay and apoptosis; open to future death-capable organs.
```
